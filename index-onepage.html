What True North does

Independent help for every decision

True North® helps aspiring home builders make informed decisions, avoid costly mistakes and move confidently from finding land through design, costing, contracts, construction and handover.

We no longer design or build. Instead we share what we learned: practical, independent guidance, proven checklists and simple tools. This is our story, and what worked for us may be useful for your situation.

    About Us

    Four decades of building, done properly

    Lucy and Tony Marshall formed True North Design Co in 1998. Their first clients had a plan with a north verandah and small leadlight windows. It would have shut out the winter sun and the summer breeze. So they started again from scratch.

    Architect John Ballinger, who oversaw the CSIRO software that became NatHERS, ran the new design through its simulations. It achieved 5 stars with single-glazed cedar windows. More than twenty years later, it still performs all year round.

    It takes the same time and materials to build a house that performs well as one that performs poorly.

    What matters is how you site the house and how you put those materials together. That is the knowledge this site is here to share.
