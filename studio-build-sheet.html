03Homepage, section by section

Build each section with Add → Sections → Blank and a grid layout. Pixel values are for the 1280 px desktop breakpoint.

Header

Sticky · white · 96 px
  • Logo image logo.png at 108 px wide, linked to Home. Beside it, the text "TRUE NORTH®" (H3) with "Independent building guidance" under it (eyebrow style).
  • Horizontal menu: Building Process · House Plans · Tools & Resources · Building Knowledge · About Us · Contact. Barlow Condensed SemiBold 16 px, UPPERCASE.
  • Yellow button "Start Here" linking to the #start anchor, plus the Site Search icon.
  • Header settings → Scroll effect: Freeze. At tablet and mobile widths, switch to a hamburger menu.
  • Top strip above the header, in a Color 5 section 36 px high: the USP line on the left (Paragraph 3, white). Facebook, Instagram and YouTube social bar on the right.

1 · Hero

2 columns · gradient Color 2 → white
  • Left column, stacked in this order:
    • Eyebrow: "For home builders, owner builders & renovators".
    • H1: "Build with knowledge, not guesswork." Put "knowledge," in Deep blue, with a yellow highlight from the text's highlight colour.
    • USP, Barlow Condensed SemiBold 24 px: "Real building experience. Independent guidance. Better decisions before costly mistakes happen."
    • Paragraph 1 lede.
    • Buttons: "Follow the 7 stages" (blue) and "Watch our story" (outline).
  • Right column: hero.jpg, 4 px radius, soft shadow. Place the compass image overlapping its bottom-left corner.
  • Below the hero, a 4-column proof strip with thin Color 3 dividers: 40+ years · No. 40 GreenSmart 1999 · 5★ → 8★ NatHERS · Award winning.

2 · Intro statement

2 columns · white
  • Left: eyebrow "What True North does" and H2 "Independent help for every decision". Right: large intro text (24 px) and the "We no longer design or build…" paragraph.

3 · Building Process (7 stages)

Color 5 background · anchor #process
  • Heading block in white text, with a yellow eyebrow.
  • Dataset → Journey Stages, sorted by stageNumber ascending, 7 items.
  • Repeater: 7 columns on desktop, 4 on tablet, and a horizontal slider on mobile. Each card:
    • Circle 48 px with a 2 px yellow border and the stageNumber text in yellow.
    • Image connected to image, 4:3.
    • title as H3 in white.
    • hint in 14 px light text.
  • Click action on the card: link to the dynamic item page (see step 04).
  • Optional: behind the circles, a dashed yellow line (a Line element at 50% opacity).

4 · Start Here

2 columns · anchor #start
  • Left: Video Player with the Snagit "Our Story" video. Until the video exists, use story.jpg as its cover image.
  • Right: H2 "New to building? Start here", then 3 numbered steps (who we are, what we did, how we can help) in a Stack.
  • Under the steps, the disclaimer box: Color 2 background with a 4 px yellow left border.

5 · Tools & Resources

Color 2 background · anchor #tools
  • Featured card on the left, 1.3 columns wide: a dataset on Tools filtered to featured = true. Image, stage chip, title, description.
  • A second dataset on Tools filtered to featured = false, sorted by stageNumber. Connect it to a repeater of white cards (2 columns, 1 px Color 3 border) showing stageLabel chip, title and description.
  • "See all tools & pricing" button links to a Tools page. Fill in price once Tony decides which tools are free and which are paid.

6 · House Plans

white · anchor #plans
  • Dataset → House Plans, sorted by sortOrder. Selection tags (All, Coastal, Bush & rural, Cabins) filter on style.
  • Repeater, 4 columns: image 4:3, title H3, tags in a chip style.
  • Below it, the copyright note box with an info icon.

7 · About Us & awards

gradient white → Color 2 · anchor #about
  • Left: the story text, a pull quote (Barlow Condensed 26 px with a 4 px blue left border), and the GreenSmart logo with membership numbers.
  • Right: dataset → Awards & History, sorted by sortOrder. A vertical repeater (1 column) makes the timeline:
    • year in blue Barlow Condensed.
    • A card with the result label, title, body and a 74 px image.
    • A 2 px Color 3 vertical line behind it.
  • Show the result label only when it equals "Winner". Use a yellow chip.

8 · Testimonials

  • Dataset → Testimonials, sorted by sortOrder. Repeater in slider layout, 1 item per view, with arrows. title in Barlow Condensed Medium 34 px white, under a yellow opening quote mark. clientName · location in mono.

04Stage pages (dynamic)

  • CMS → Journey Stages → Add dynamic page → Item page. Set the URL to /building-process/{title}.
  • Layout: "Stage {stageNumber} of 7" eyebrow, headline as H1, intro, topics as a text list, and the image connected to image.
  • Two cards:
    • "Free PDF guide": guideTitle with a button linked to guideUrl.
    • Video Player linked to videoUrl, captioned with videoTitle.
  • Hide both buttons when their field is empty.
  • Previous / Next buttons: use the dataset's Previous/Next item actions.
  • Upload each lead-magnet PDF to Media Manager, then paste its link into guideUrl in the CMS.
